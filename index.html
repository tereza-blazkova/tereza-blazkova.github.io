About

Hi! I am a PhD student in Social Data Science at the University of Copenhagen. My research interest is developing and evaluating predictive systems with an emphasis on aligning technical capabilities with societal needs.My current work centers on predictive analytics in education (dropout early warning systems), on algorithmic fairness, and participatory algorithm design. I am also interested in AI governance and computational social science, broadly conceived.
